World

Working worldbuilding notes; details may evolve.

Harrowmere

A crowded industrial city built along a deep canyon. Stone bridges connect neighborhoods, lifts carry people between levels, and underground reservoirs provide heat. Reliable transport and warmth reach wealthy districts first; lower neighborhoods face shortages and breakdowns.

Resonance

Resonants connect to an invisible magical field to influence a substance or force. Most awaken one primary discipline; a weaker secondary affinity is uncommon. Power competes with range and precision, and overuse causes fatigue or injury.

Everyday life

Trams, radios, workshops, schools, and apprenticeships shape daily life. Pattern engines follow arranged instructions, allowing inventions and games like those Retton enjoys. People without resonance remain essential inventors, workers, and leaders.

Power and inequality

A city council governs Harrowmere, but guilds control training licenses, jobs, and essential services. People without resonance have legal rights while facing unequal access to education and well-paid work. Varen exploits those real grievances.

The Lantern Network

A working name for an independent coalition that helps neighborhoods and investigates abuses. Its members bring different abilities, backgrounds, and views.

Beyond the city

Neighboring cities trade food, fuel, and metals. Their cultures and governments differ; nations are not divided by magical element, and no chosen person automatically masters every power.

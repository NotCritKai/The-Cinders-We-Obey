Characters

Story-development profiles contain spoilers. New preference details and introduction chapters are working proposals.

Sixteen · Kindlecraft

Retton

Reserved at first, playful and sarcastic with trusted people. Creative, competitive, and anxious about belonging.

Age
16
Power
Kindlecraft (heatcraft). Former prodigy rebuilding reliability and fundamentals.
Likes
Programming pattern engines, experiments, inventing games, friendly competition, and learning through practice.
Dislikes
Repetitive tasks, harsh criticism, feeling excluded, and being treated as incapable.
Goal
To belong, be valued, and find meaningful work without helping harm others.
Blind spot
Fear of losing Gray makes him trust Gray’s judgment above his own.
Relationships
Gray is his mentor and chosen older brother. Tarin is an old friend who shares his projects and offers room to disagree. His parents were present but emotionally inattentive.
Everyday habits
Teases and talks freely when comfortable; becomes quieter when hurt. Can lose interest in routine but become absorbed in a project.
Nineteen · Kindlecraft

Gray

A gifted, disciplined mentor whose sincere affection for Retton coexists with possessiveness and deception.

Age
19
Power
Kindlecraft. More naturally gifted and consistently trained than Retton.
Likes
Games, teasing, interactive training, helping Retton improve, and feeling needed.
Dislikes
Losing Retton’s trust, being challenged about his authority, and uncertainty he cannot control.
Goal
To keep Retton close and safe according to his own judgment.
Blind spot
He treats care as permission to control and withhold the truth.
Relationships
Retton is his chosen younger brother. Varen personally saved his life, creating a powerful sense of debt. Tarin is a genuine friend whose independence can feel threatening.
Everyday habits
Makes practice engaging and usually eases off teasing when he notices discomfort. Conceals guilt and rationalizes harmful choices as protection.
Eighteen · Tidecraft / Flowwork

Tarin

A practical apprentice mechanic and longtime friend of both boys. Dry humor, stubborn independence, and familiar routines make him an anchor outside the factions.

Age
18 (working choice)
Skills
Tidecraft resonant specializing in Flowwork: precise currents, cooling, hydraulic mechanisms, and leak detection. Needs available water and mechanical knowledge; cannot control a machine simply because it contains water. Repairs tools and pattern engines through learned engineering skill.
Likes
Shared games, building with Retton, repairing worn machines, and keeping small promises.
Dislikes
People speaking for others, secrets that put friends at risk, and guild barriers to opportunity.
Goal
To earn enough to open a workshop independent of the guilds.
Blind spot
Withdraws when hurt and expects others to understand what they did wrong without being told.
Relationships
Retton is a creative collaborator and old friend. Gray is someone he genuinely cares about but will challenge. He belongs to their friendship before either faction.
Role in the story
His workshop provides a familiar place to return to. He offers help without demanding immediate disclosure, but refuses to cover for Gray’s lies. He is an anchor, not a cure.
Everyday habits
Shows affection through repairs and remembered preferences. Argues comfortably with Retton, allowing disagreement without making friendship conditional.
Director · Affinity undecided

Varen

Composed, patient, and politically ambitious. He believes his rule helps people while knowingly making freedom depend on obedience.

Age
Not decided; current portrait suggests late forties to early fifties.
Power
Affinity undecided. His authority comes from institutions, choices, and deception, not an obedience power.
Likes
Order, patient planning, useful loyalty, and presenting himself as a stabilizing influence.
Dislikes
Dissent that threatens his authority, losing useful followers, and limits on his control.
Goal
To replace existing inequality with political order under his own leadership.
Blind spot
Believes the benefits of his rule justify denying others a genuine choice.
Relationships
Gray feels indebted to him after being saved. Retton encounters his agenda mostly through Gray. He leads both factions, hiding behind a figurehead in the second.
Everyday habits
Speaks calmly, works through intermediaries, and reassures others while keeping crucial information private.
Early forties · Non-resonant

Mara Venn

Tarin’s workshop supervisor: practical, impatient with shortcuts, and quietly generous.

Age
Early forties (working choice)
Skills
Non-resonant mechanic and workshop supervisor. Maintains equipment, trains apprentices, and manages repairs.
Likes
Safe workmanship, honest estimates, useful inventions, and apprentices who follow through.
Dislikes
Concealed mistakes, reckless shortcuts, and guild restrictions that squeeze small workshops.
Goal
Keep the workshop licensed, solvent, and useful to its neighborhood.
Blind spot
Her blunt standards can sound like personal criticism, particularly to Retton.
Relationships
Tarin’s supervisor. Offers Retton a paid repair job requiring reliable heatcraft. Expects Gray to help without covering up errors.
Opening role
The trio’s repair mistake leaves her accountable to a customer. Her threatened workshop license introduces guild inequality through a concrete problem.
Seventeen · Galecraft

Iven Dorr

A sociable, competitive courier whose steady practice makes him an uncomfortable comparison for Retton.

Age
17 (working choice)
Power
Galecraft, used for controlled gusts and softened landings while delivering across the city.
Likes
Fast routes, friendly competition, neighborhood gossip, and doing a job well.
Dislikes
Missed deliveries, being accused of bad intentions, and unreliable equipment.
Goal
Build a reliable courier reputation and earn better routes.
Blind spot
Assumes familiar teasing and high expectations land the same way they used to.
Relationships
Knew Retton when he was considered a prodigy. Delivers to Mara’s workshop and crosses paths with Tarin and Gray.
Opening role
Brings a guild notice threatening the workshop’s license. His rivalry with Retton creates friction without making him secretly cruel.

Glossary

Working worldbuilding notes; details may evolve.

18 terms

Alloywork
Advanced Stonecraft techniques for shaping and moving familiar metals.
Anchor
A practitioner of Gravcraft.
Concordance
Working name for the experimental headpiece that distorts emotional associations and judgment. It leaves remembered events intact.
Echocraft
Sound and vibration resonance: signals, motion sensing, damping, and tuned fracture.
Galecraft
Air and pressure resonance: gusts, pressure pulses, gliding, and softened falls.
Gleamcraft
Light resonance: glare, concealment, and silent images without physical substance.
Gravcraft
Local weight manipulation, limited by mass, field size, and duration.
Harrowmere
The industrial canyon city where the story is set.
Kindlecraft
Fire and heat resonance. Commonly called heatcraft; practiced by Retton and Gray.
Lantern Network
Working name for the independent civic coalition that supports neighborhoods.
Pattern engine
A machine programmed through arranged instructions; useful for automation, inventions, and games.
Resonance
The connection to the world’s magical field that enables a discipline.
Resonant
A person able to use resonance.
Rimework
Advanced Tidecraft techniques for freezing and thawing limited water by transferring heat.
Sparkcraft
Electrical resonance: collecting charge, guiding arcs, and interacting with exposed machinery.
Stonecraft
Earth resonance: moving soil, shaping stone, and exploiting structural weak points.
Tidecraft
Water resonance: moving existing water into currents, shields, and jets.
Verdantcraft
Plant resonance: guiding living growth, roots, and vines with available resources.
